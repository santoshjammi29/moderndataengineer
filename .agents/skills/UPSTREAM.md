The `pyspark-handbook` skill is authored from [santoshjammi29/pyspark-handbook](https://github.com/santoshjammi29/pyspark-handbook), pinned to commit `bb56d371a6c1c14b92e3ae0336e15b7784f75107` (2026-11-02). It includes all 105 source notebooks, Databricks deployment templates, project dependency files, a notebook catalog, and its source license.

The imported source skill and resource content is otherwise preserved from those snapshots. The PySpark skill entrypoint and catalog are authored additions; they do not claim that the notebooks have been executed or validated in a target workspace.
